Act as a senior Android architect, Kotlin developer, Firebase engineer, AI integration specialist, and world-class UI/UX designer.

Build a complete production-level Android application called:

“Namma-Pustaka: AI-Powered Smart Rural Library Assistant”

====================================================
PROJECT VISION
====================================================

The application is a smart digital library system for rural schools.

The app transforms traditional school libraries into intelligent digital reading ecosystems.

The goals are:
- Digitize village school libraries
- Improve reading culture
- Track book borrowing
- Help students discover books
- Help teachers manage books efficiently
- Encourage literacy through gamification
- Enable AI-powered summaries and recommendations

====================================================
CORE PROBLEMS TO SOLVE
====================================================

1. Rural libraries have no proper digital catalog.
2. Students do not know available books.
3. Teachers struggle to track borrowed books.
4. Books get lost frequently.
5. Reading culture is weak due to lack of engagement.
6. No analytics about student reading habits.
